# Matching Rubric V2

## A. JD requirement level

### 高要求
Use when the JD:
- states a core job responsibility
- uses mandatory language such as `必须`, `熟练掌握`
- repeats/emphasizes the capability
- would materially determine screening

### 中要求
Use when the JD:
- states an important supporting responsibility
- says `优先`, `熟练掌握优先`
- materially improves fit but is not a hard gate

### 低要求
Use when the JD:
- says `了解`, `具备...意识`, nice-to-have
- is broad/background context with limited screening weight

Do not decide importance from sentence order alone.

## B. Resume evidence grade

### A — 强证据：明确熟练/反复验证
The capability is explicitly stated as proficient/mastered and is credible in context, or appears repeatedly across relevant experiences.

### B — 强证据：直接行为证明
The capability is directly demonstrated through concrete action, method/tool, ownership, or result.

### C — 中等证据
Relevant content exists but proof is incomplete or not fully aligned.

### D — 弱证据
The exact capability is absent, but adjacent/transferable evidence exists.

### E — 无证据
No relevant evidence exists in supplied materials.

## C. JD × evidence mapping

| JD要求 | 强证据 A/B | 中证据 C | 弱证据 D | 无证据 E |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 高 | 强匹配 | 部分匹配 | 缺少证据 | 明显缺口 |
| 中 | 优于需求 | 强匹配 | 部分匹配 | 明显缺口 |
| 低 | 优于需求 | 优于需求 | 可忽略 | 可忽略 |

Use the table deterministically unless a hard eligibility rule applies.

## D. Hard gate rule

Hard gates are evaluated separately as:
- 满足
- 不满足
- 待确认

A hard-gate failure cannot be converted into `部分匹配` by unrelated strengths.

## E. Evidence-chain rule

For important behavioral requirements, prefer evidence shaped like:

`Action → Method/Tool → Business Context → Result`

Not every element is mandatory, but the missing element should influence evidence grade.

Example:

JD: "根据数据优化频道转化"

Resume: "熟练 Excel"

Result:
- Tool keyword exists
- No business analysis/action/result
- Do not give A/B
- likely D or E depending on other materials

## F. Multi-source rule

Primary evidence and supporting evidence may be combined only when they refer to the same capability.

Do not combine:
- one role's tool skill
- another role's unrelated result
- a third role's ownership

into a fictional single experience.

## G. Rationale format

One sentence:
`JD要求什么 → 简历有什么证据 → 缺什么 → 因此为什么是该等级`
